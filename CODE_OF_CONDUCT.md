# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

FarmEase is committed to a welcoming, respectful community for people with every level of agricultural and software experience.

We expect contributors to be considerate, use inclusive language, give and receive constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for farmers and the community. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Report concerns privately through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
